PRODUCT NOTES

Aria Wireless Headphones
Over-ear, active noise cancelling, 32 hour battery, USB-C charging, Bluetooth
5.3, multipoint pairing with two devices. Works with iOS, Android, Windows and
macOS. 24 month warranty.

Aria Earbuds Pro
In-ear, noise cancelling, 7 hours per charge and 28 with the case, IPX4 sweat
resistance. Hygiene seal must be intact for a return. 24 month warranty.

Nomad Bluetooth Speaker
IP67 waterproof, 18 hour battery, pairs two speakers for stereo, USB-C. Not
suitable for submersion beyond 1 metre or 30 minutes.

Pulse Fitness Band
Heart rate, sleep tracking, 10 day battery. Companion app on iOS 15+ and
Android 10+.

Pulse Watch 2
AMOLED display, GPS, 5 day battery, water resistant to 50 metres. Works with
both iOS and Android; some notification features are Android only.

Aura Smart Lamp
Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only, 16 million colours, works with the Aura app, Google Home and
Apple Home. Does not support 5 GHz networks.

Vault Power Bank 20k
20,000 mAh, 65 W USB-C output, charges a laptop. Airline safe under the 100 Wh
limit.

Forge Mechanical Keyboard
Hot-swappable switches, wired or Bluetooth, per-key backlight. Keycaps are
standard profile and can be replaced.

Clarity 27 inch Monitor
4K, 60 Hz, USB-C with 90 W power delivery, height adjustable stand, VESA 100
compatible. One year dead-pixel guarantee under the standard warranty.
