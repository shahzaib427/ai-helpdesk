FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long do I have to return something?
30 days from delivery. See the return policy for condition requirements.

How do I track my order?
Open the order in your account, or use the tracking number in your dispatch
email. Tracking can take 24 hours to update after dispatch.

When will I get my refund?
Within 3 working days of the return reaching our warehouse, plus 5-10 working
days for your bank to post it.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
Only while the order is pending or processing. After dispatch the carrier
controls the address.

Do you ship internationally?
Yes, to 24 countries. Estimates run from 1-2 working days inside the Netherlands
to 7-14 working days for the rest of the world.

Is shipping free?
Yes on orders over 75 USD. Below that it is 6.95 USD in the EU and 14.95 USD
elsewhere.

My item stopped working. What now?
If it is within 24 months of delivery, this is a warranty claim. Contact support
with your order number and a description of the fault.

I was charged twice.
Contact support with both transaction dates. Duplicate charges are urgent and
are refunded within 1 working day of confirmation.

Can I talk to a person?
Yes. Ask for a human at any point in the chat and we will hand the conversation
to an agent, along with everything you have already told us.

Do you price match?
No. If the price of something you bought drops within 14 days, contact support
and we will refund the difference on that order.
