NORTHWIND ELECTRONICS - COMPANY INFORMATION

About us
Northwind Electronics is an online retailer of audio, wearable and home
technology. We were founded in 2016 and ship to 24 countries from warehouses in
Rotterdam, Toronto and Singapore.

Support hours
Live chat with our AI assistant: 24 hours a day, every day.
Human support agents: Monday to Friday, 08:00-20:00 UTC, and Saturday
09:00-17:00 UTC. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays in the
Netherlands.

Response targets
First reply to a ticket: within 4 working hours.

1 working hour.
