SHIPPING POLICY

Dispatch time
Orders placed before 14:00 UTC on a working day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed later, at the weekend, or on a Dutch public holiday are dispatched
on the next working day.

Delivery estimates from dispatch
Netherlands and Belgium: 1-2 working days
Rest of the EU and the UK: 2-4 working days
Canada and the USA: 4-7 working days
Rest of the world: 7-14 working days

Shipping cost
Free standard shipping on orders over 75 USD. Below that, standard shipping is
6.95 USD within the EU and 14.95 USD elsewhere. Express shipping is 19.95 USD
and cuts the estimate roughly in half.

Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking can
take up to 24 hours to show movement. Order status in your account is the same
information the support team sees.

Delayed parcels
A parcel is considered delayed once it is 3 working days past the latest
delivery estimate. Contact support and we will open a carrier investigation,
which takes up to 5 working days. If the parcel is not found we resend or refund.

Parcels marked delivered but not received
Check with neighbours and look for a carrier card first. If the parcel is still
missing 24 hours later, contact support. We treat these as urgent and will not
ask you to wait longer than 48 hours before acting.

Address changes
An address can be changed only while the order status is still pending or
processing. Once it ships, the address is fixed by the carrier.

Customs and duties
Orders shipped outside the EU may attract import duty, which is payable by the
recipient and is not included in our prices.
