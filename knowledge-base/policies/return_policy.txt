RETURN POLICY

Return window
You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The
30 days are counted from the date the carrier marks the parcel as delivered.

Condition
Items must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories and
manuals. Items showing damage beyond normal inspection may receive a partial
refund of up to 70% of the purchase price.

Items that cannot be returned
- In-ear headphones and earbuds where the hygiene seal has been broken
- Gift cards
- Software licences and digital download codes
- Items marked "final sale" on the product page

How to start a return
1. Sign in and open the order in your account.
2. Select "Return item" and choose a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label we email you, or ask us to send a QR code instead.
4. Drop the parcel at any GlobalPost collection point within 14 days.

Return shipping cost
Returns are free within the EU, the UK and Canada. For all other destinations a
flat fee of 12 USD is deducted from the refund, unless the item arrived damaged
or we sent the wrong item, in which case we pay return shipping.

Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new
order. If the replacement price has risen since your original order, contact
support and we will honour the original price for 14 days.
