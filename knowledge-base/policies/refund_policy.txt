REFUND POLICY

Processing time
Refunds are issued once the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes
inspection, normally within 3 working days of arrival. The money then takes
5-10 working days to appear, depending on your bank.

Refund method
Refunds go back to the original payment method. If that card has expired or been
closed, contact support and we will arrange a bank transfer. We cannot refund to
an account belonging to someone else.

What is refunded
The item price and any original standard shipping charge are refunded in full.
Express shipping upgrades are not refunded unless the item was faulty or we made
an error.

Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same order, contact support with both
transaction dates. Duplicate charges are treated as urgent and are refunded
within 1 working day of being confirmed. Do not place a replacement order until
the duplicate is resolved.

Partial refunds
A partial refund may apply when an item is returned outside its original
packaging, with accessories missing, or with damage that is not a manufacturing
fault. We always tell you the amount before processing it.

Cancelled orders
An order cancelled before it ships is refunded in full within 2 working days. An
order that has already shipped must be returned under the return policy.
