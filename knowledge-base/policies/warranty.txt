WARRANTY

Standard cover
Every product carries a 24 month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date,
except accessories such as cables, cases and stands, which carry 12 months.

What is covered
Manufacturing defects, components that fail under normal use, battery capacity
falling below 60% of its rated capacity within the warranty period, and firmware
faults that we cannot resolve remotely.

What is not covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic wear, damage from third-party
chargers or unapproved repairs, and loss or theft.

Making a claim
Contact support with the order number and a description of the fault. For most
faults we ask for a short video. We then either send a replacement, repair the
item, or refund it, at our discretion. Replacements ship within 3 working days of
a claim being approved.

Warranty on replacements
A replacement carries the remainder of the original warranty, or 6 months,
whichever is longer.

Proof of purchase
The order in your account is proof of purchase. You do not need a paper receipt.
